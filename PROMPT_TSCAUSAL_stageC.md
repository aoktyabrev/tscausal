## The question of the stage

Do physically meaningful processes violate N1 and N2, and which ones exactly. The central
hypothesis: N1 and N2 express the consistency of the causal order between the
directions of time. Then they should be violated by processes with an **indefinite
direction of time**, but not by processes with an indefinite order at a definite
direction. If so, this is the answer to question 3 of the Mrini–Hardy paper
(arXiv:2406.18489) in the restricted class.

## C.1 — calibration of the SDP (gate)

1. Time-forward process matrices (Oreshkov–Costa–Brukner), qubits: the maximum of
   GYNI and LGYNI. Take the reference numbers from Branciard et al., NJP 18, 013008 (2016),
   with a quotation, not from memory. Agreement to within the solver's tolerance is a gate.
2. The same SDP with postselection allowed, in the TS formalism, on N1 and N2 must
   give the algebraic maximum (the paper's claim). If it does not — there is an error
   in the formalism, stop.
3. Classical TS circuits: the maximum of N1 and N2 equals the bound (by construction, from B.2).
4. The solver is tested on a problem with a known answer and in a regime where it is
   obliged to report failure (for example, a deliberately infeasible constraint).
   The lesson from SelfDual: a solver failure is easily taken for a physical gap.

## C.2 — the hierarchy of processes on N1 and N2

For each class — the maximum of N1 and N2 (and of all 6 closure classes) with
system dimensions qubit, then qutrit if the size of the SDP allows:

- **P1** — quantum circuits with a definite order and a definite direction,
  without postselection. Expectation by construction: they do not violate. This is the control.
- **P2** — indefinite order, definite direction: time-forward OCB process matrices
  embedded into the TS formalism without postselection, including the quantum switch as a
  particular case.
- **P3** — an indefinite direction of time without postselection: the two-party analogue
  of the quantum time flip (Chiribella–Liu). The construction has to be built explicitly:
  a control qubit coherently choosing the forward or the backward
  direction for both laboratories (variants: a common control for both; independent
  controls). Describe each variant and check that it belongs to the restricted class
  of C.0.4.
- **P4** — the entire restricted class (an SDP over all admissible TS processes without
  postselection). An upper bound for P1–P3.

For every violation: exhibit the explicit process matrix and instruments,
recompute p(a,b,x,y) by a direct run, independently of the SDP, in exact or
extended precision. A violation not confirmed by a direct run does not count.

## C.3 — a test of consistency, not only of magnitude

If P3 violates N1 or N2, check that the violation is connected precisely with the
indefiniteness of the direction: under dephasing of the control qubit
(an incoherent mixture of "forward" and "backward") the violation must disappear. If it
remains — the matter is not the coherence of the direction, and the central hypothesis in this
form is wrong.
