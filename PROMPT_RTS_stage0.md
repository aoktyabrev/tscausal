A new branch, a continuation of TSCAUSAL (lemmas D6, D8) and [[quantum-reconstruction]] (the dispute
of Renou against Hoffreumon–Woods). The rules are unchanged: Rule 0, preregistration as a separate

## The question

Hoffreumon–Woods (arXiv:2603.19208, March 2026): under operational independence of the sources,
real QM (RQT) reproduces any network correlations (Theorem 1) and any finite sequential protocols
with channels and measurements (Theorem 2). The mechanism: the states of the sources in the RQT
model are not products, but are locally indistinguishable from them; the canonical example is
¼(I⊗I − αJ⊗J) with antisymmetric J (Caves et al.).

In the time-symmetric regime without selection (Mrini–Hardy, the class ISO) the global past is
maximally mixed, and the admissible terms of the process matrix are restricted by linear conditions
(for two parties: only 1, A_O B_I, A_I B_O — lemma D6).

**Does the hidden correlation required by the RQT model fit into the real class ISO?**
- No → under fundamental time symmetry RQT is refutable where HW's Theorem 2 says that without it
  it is irrefutable. i is necessary because of the symmetry of time.
- Yes → the HW theorem carries over to the TS regime; that is a result too (it closes the question).

## R.1 — the translation of the binocal scenario into TS without selection (gate)

Following the pattern of D8: sources → identity channels from a maximally mixed past, the roles of
the settings and the outcomes as in D8. Construct a complex ISO process for three parties
(A, B, C; B has two inputs) reproducing Renou's correlations.

Calibrations:
- the complex ISO process gives the complex value of T from Renou's paper;
- real product processes (W = W₁ ⊗ W₂, both real) give no more than Renou's real bound (see-saw, at
  least 50 starts) — a reproduction of their result in our language;
- membership in ISO is checked by two independent routes, as in T3.0.

## R.2 — the structure of the terms in the real basis (the main item)

1. The null space of the linear ISO conditions for this configuration in a basis containing
   antisymmetric generators (of J type). Which types of terms with J factors are admissible?
   Separately: are terms linking the two channels admissible (4-party, one J factor per each
   channel), and in what form.
2. Translate the HW RQT model for the binocal scenario into the process language. Does it lie in
   real ISO? If not — which term takes it out, and can that term be replaced by an admissible one
   without loss of operational independence.
3. Define operational independence at the level of processes: the absence of observable correlations
   between the channels under any local TS operations. Check that terms similar to
   ¼(I⊗I − αJ⊗J), if they are admissible, satisfy it.

## R.3 — a direct test

The maximum of T over real ISO processes with operational independence of the channels (see-saw + an
SDP relaxation from above, where possible). Compare with the complex value.
- it agrees → HW carries over to TS;
- strictly less, with an upper bound separated from the complex value → a candidate for the result
  "time symmetry makes RQT refutable"; next — a proof.

Any claimed discrepancy is confirmed by an upper bound (not only a see-saw) and by a recomputation
in exact or extended precision.
