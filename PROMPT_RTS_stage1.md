Stage RTS 0 is closed. What is firm: a dual certificate ≤ 3√2 with the HW operations; the analysis
of the terms that take the HW construction out of ISO (the marginal ones: J_A′J_C′ is forbidden in
TF, J_B1′J_B2′ is forbidden in TB); the cleanup correction (factor-wise noise instead of the white

Open: the value with free operations. See-saw gives 6.869154 at (4,4,4,4); the thresholds are
7.6605 (Renou's real bound) and 6√2 ≈ 8.4853 (the complex value).

**This is the last computational stage of the branch.** The budget is no more than one day of
computation in total. After it the branch is closed in whatever shape it is in, and we move on to
the write-up. The rules are unchanged: Rule 0, preregistration as a separate commit before the first
run, `RESULTS.md` generated by a script, anti-vacuum tests, a recomputation in exact or extended
precision.

## S.1 — the dimension scan (the main item)

The same see-saw with free operations, scenario D12, at (2,2,2,2), (4,4,4,4), (6,6,6,6), (8,8,8,8).
For each dimension:
- at least 20 successful starts (at (8,8,8,8) — as many as the budget allows, but at least 5);
- the final point is cleaned with factor-wise noise that preserves ISO and OI exactly;
- into the table: raw 𝒯, cleaned 𝒯, λmin, the residual of the ISO marginals, the OI violation, the
  number of successful and failed starts, the time.

If (8,8,8,8) does not fit in memory or time — record the resource ceiling as a number (the size of
the SDP block, the memory, the time per iteration) and do not substitute extrapolation for it.

A warm start from the embedding of a point of smaller dimension is allowed, but then a cold start
for the same dimension is obligatory as well, and in the report they are separated.

The interpretation fixed in advance:
- 𝒯 grows monotonically towards 8.4853 → the OI loophole works in the TS regime too, all it needs
  is dimension; the conclusion "HW carries over" as numerical evidence;
- 𝒯 reaches a plateau (two or three neighbouring dimensions within 1e−3) noticeably below
  7.6605 → numerical evidence that time symmetry cuts off the loophole; the result is presented as
  evidence, not as a proof;
- 𝒯 exceeds 7.6605 → a separate check of the point (admissibility, OI, a recomputation in mpmath),
  and this changes the branch's conclusion; report immediately.

## S.2 — an upper bound with free operations (on the remainder of the budget)

One attempt, within the same one-day budget:

  parties and of local relabellings), in order to shrink the 256×256 block;

  cross-check it with SCS at a common point;

  factorisation, as in R.2.

If the bound does not work out — record exactly that: "there is no upper bound with free
operations", indicating what precisely got in the way (the size of the block, the memory, the time).
