## T3.1.c — which "mirrors" work

The group of local involutions of the values for three binary parties: inversion of each bit at each
party, on the input and on the output separately; permutations of the parties; the exchange
input↔output (time reversal in the sense of Mrini–Hardy). For Lugano and for each element g of the
group, check:
- whether ½(Lugano + g·Lugano) lies in ISO₃;
- whether the mixture is causally separable (an exact certificate, as in T3.0);
- for which g both conditions hold simultaneously.

Then the same for averaging over subgroups (a mixture over the whole orbit). The goal is to
understand whether the "all onto all" inversion is the only mirror that works, and what the ones
that work have in common.
